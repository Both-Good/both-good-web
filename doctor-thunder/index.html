What is it?

A block-breaking puzzle, deckbuilding roguelike. You are DOCTOR THUNDER. Treat your patients, make number go up, and maybe, just maybe, become human again.

After the falls of man and machine, a few rust buckets still manage to survive this post-post-apocalyptic world. Siphon their energy, bolstering your own power in hopes of becoming human again.

How it plays

  • Place & clear: drop pieces onto your patient's grid and fill lines to clear them.
  • Store volts: every clear charges you up. Hit the round goal before you run out of pieces, survive as the goals keep climbing.
  • Batteries & hacks: between rounds, shop for upgrades that drastically change how you play and enable you to generate more and more volts.
  • Build your run: find synergies, stack them, break the game.
  • Unlocks: access stronger batteries, stranger patients, harsher symptoms and new difficulties as you go.
