What is it?

Golftacular! is mini‑golf… barely. It blends party‑game chaos and dumb fun with mini‑golf precision and strategy. Up to 4 players take turns as Tortillo, Chuck, Fin, Poop, Papa, Mama, Claude, and Frend, each aiming for the hole while unleashing mayhem on anyone in the way.

Dozens of handcrafted courses and tons of possible item combos. Call in airstrikes, play rock‑paper‑scissors, give your ball wings, place portals and so much more.

  • Items, items, items: stack wild abilities for game‑breaking combos.
  • Ear Candy Radio: an original soundtrack by Baba (@babamadethis).
  • Pass & play: short on controllers? Share one.
  • Solo puzzles: no friends? No problem. Solve challenging courses on your own.
  • Play your way: full controller and keyboard / mouse support.
